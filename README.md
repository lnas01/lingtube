---

## ✨ Core Features

* **📝 Bilingual Subtitles**
    Watch with your target language and native language side by side. Read, compare, and understand instantly — no more pausing to guess what was said.

* **🧩 Smart Sentence Merging**
    Auto-generated captions are often broken into choppy, meaningless fragments. LingTube intelligently stitches them back into complete, natural sentences so you learn how the language is really structured.

* **🏷️ Part-of-Speech Tagging**
    Every word is color-coded by its grammatical role — *nouns, verbs, adjectives, and more* — so sentence structure becomes visual and intuitive across English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, and Korean.

* **🖍️ Select-to-Translate (Any Length)**
    This isn't just single-word lookup. Highlight anything — a word, a phrase, a full sentence, or several lines — and get an instant, accurate translation in a clean pop-up. Select exactly what you don't understand, at any length, and save it to your personal vocabulary list with one tap.

* **🔍 Grammar Analysis**
    Confused by a tricky sentence? Let AI break it down clause by clause, explaining the grammar and why it's built that way — like having a private tutor for every line.

* **🗣️ Text-to-Speech (TTS)**
    Listen to natural AI-powered pronunciation of any subtitle line. Supports multiple languages and voices.

* **🎙️ Video Transcription**
    No captions? Inaccurate captions? Awkward, unnatural translations? LingTube uses advanced speech recognition to regenerate clean, natural, native-sounding subtitles from the original audio — so you can study any video with captions you can actually trust. *(Available on YouTube.)*

* **🧠 Summary & Outline**
    Get the big picture in seconds. LingTube generates concise AI summaries and visual mind-map outlines of any video, so you grasp the key ideas before — or after — you dive in.

* **🤖 AI Assistant**
    Ask anything about the video. Your built-in AI companion answers questions, explains context, clarifies cultural references, and helps you go deeper — right in the sidebar.

* **🗣️ Shadowing Practice**
    Improve pronunciation and fluency by repeating after native speakers, sentence by sentence. Record your voice, compare your waveform to the original, and hear your progress.

* **📚 AI Word & Phrase Insights**
    Go beyond simple translations. LingTube provides rich explanations of words and phrases — meanings, usage, nuance, and examples — powered by AI so you truly understand, not just memorize.

* **💬 Scenario-Based AI Conversations**
    Practice speaking in realistic, everyday scenarios. Chat with an AI partner, get instant writing corrections and feedback, and build the confidence to use what you've learned in the real world.

* **🎉 Danmaku (Bullet Comments)**
    Learning is better together. Share notes and reactions that fly across the screen, discover insights from fellow learners, and turn solo study into a lively shared experience.
